Who we are

MASAN Inc. is a residential assisted living company founded by five partners. We buy and operate small, licensed assisted living homes, starting in Arizona.

Why we started MASAN

Good assisted living is small, personal, and steady. But too many small homes struggle on their own: thin staffing, paperwork piling up, and owners doing everything at once. We started MASAN to give those homes a stronger footing, with the people, systems, and standards to run them well for the long term.

We are operators first. We stay close to the homes, the staff, and the residents, because that is where the care actually happens.

What we believe

  • Residents first

    Every decision starts with the person living in the home. Care plans, staffing, and daily routines are built around residents, not around convenience.

  • Small homes

    Ten beds or fewer, in a real house in a real neighborhood. Small is not a limitation. It is what makes the care personal.

  • Licensed and compliant

    We operate inside the rules, not around them. Licensing, training, documentation, and safety are the floor, not the ceiling.

  • Stable teams

    Residents do best with caregivers they know. We invest in our people, schedule fairly, and keep the same faces showing up.

Where we operate

MASAN Inc. is a Virginia corporation. Our first homes are in Arizona, where we are acquiring existing licensed homes and building the team to run them well.
