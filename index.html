What we do

We buy and run small assisted living homes, and we do it the way it should be done: a real home, a stable team, and care that starts with the person.

We acquire

We buy existing, licensed, operating assisted living homes, and we keep the good things that made them home: the residents, the staff, and the routines that work.

We operate

We run our homes day to day with trained local teams, clear standards, and owners who stay close to the work, not far from it.

We care

Personal care, supervisory care, and directed care, delivered in a small home setting where every resident is known, not just counted.

A home, not a facility

Our homes are small by design, ten beds or fewer. That is the whole point. A small home means caregivers who know each resident, meals around a real table, and a place that feels like living somewhere, not staying somewhere.

Every home we run is licensed, staffed by certified managers and trained caregivers, and held to the same standard we would want for our own family.
