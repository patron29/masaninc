What we do

Three things, done well: care for residents, run homes properly, and give good homes a future.

Resident care

Personal care, supervisory care, and directed care in a small home setting. Help with bathing, dressing, meals, and medication, plus daily routines and activities that keep residents engaged and connected.

Home operations

Staffing, training, and scheduling. Medication management and service plans. Licensing, compliance, and safety. Meals, housekeeping, and maintenance. The unglamorous work that makes good care possible.

Acquisitions

We buy existing licensed assisted living homes. For owners considering a sale, we offer a straightforward process, a fair deal, and a team that keeps the home running and the residents cared for.

Care in a real home

Our homes have ten beds or fewer. Residents live in a house, not a ward: a kitchen, a living room, a yard, and caregivers who know their names, their routines, and their families.

Care is planned around each resident, with service plans reviewed regularly and families kept in the loop.
